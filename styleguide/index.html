Motion

Calm, physical and short. Things settle into place like paper on a board; the one flourish is the seal stamp. Fast 150ms (hover, press), base 200ms (modal, slide), slow 350ms (list entry), stamp 560ms. Entrances ease out; the switch thumb settles with a gentle ease and the stamp overshoots once in its own keyframes. Reduced-motion removes all of it.

Rules

  • One flourish per view. Everything else is quiet feedback that answers an action.
  • Move 10px at most, except the stamp. Ease out; never ease in on entrances.
  • Animate transform, opacity and shadow only; never layout.
  • Stagger only on first load. Never re-run entrances on content people are reading.
  • Nothing loops except spinner and skeleton. Reduced-motion removes all of it.

Buttons

One primary per view. Dialog footers: outline cancel, then primary confirm. Icon-only needs aria-label.

    Menus, popovers & tooltips

    Pick by job. Menu: a list of actions. Popover: a little extra content or a mini form. Tooltip: a name or shortcut for an icon button, never required information. Confirm dialog: before something that can't be undone.

    海报比例

    3:4 适合朋友圈与打印，9:16 适合手机全屏。

    分享 撤销 ⌘Z

    Menus: Arrow keys, Home/End, Esc returns focus to the button. Tooltips appear after 350ms on hover or keyboard focus and are hidden on touch devices. The tooltip is a hint only: the button already has an aria-label.

    Page layouts

    Three shells cover almost every screen. Content width is ds-container (72rem, 24px gutter); page titles are serif, with the primary action first on phones.

    Content & voice

    Warm, plain and respectful, like a helpful neighbour at the community centre. Chinese first, English beside or beneath. Say what happened and what to do next; never blame the person.

    Writing rules

    • Buttons: verb + object. Cancel is always 取消 / Cancel.
    • Errors: what happened, then the fix. No codes up front, no "Oops".
    • Empty states: what belongs here, plus one action.
    • Success: short and specific. The seal stamp is for the big ones only.
    • Chinese: full-width punctuation (，。：). English: sentence case, never ALL CAPS sentences.
    • Space between Chinese and numbers or Latin words (更新于 3 分钟前).
    • Dates: 2026年10月5日 / 5 Oct 2026. Don't translate proper names.
    • Icon-only buttons carry an aria-label in the page language.
